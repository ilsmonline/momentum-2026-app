OUR WORSHIP TEAM

RIVER VALLEY WORSHIP

River Valley Worship is a collective of worship pastors, songwriters and musicians from River Valley Church in the Twin Cities, creating artful, congregational and Spirit-filled music.

Read About the Band +

Based out of River Valley Church in the Twin Cities, River Valley Worship seeks to pioneer new sonic landscapes for the Church on both local and global levels.

The band's 2018 release Million Lifetimes, their first record with BEC Recordings, included the Billboard Christian Top 25 anthem Hope Has A Name. Their subsequent projects include Altars and Attention EP.

In June 2022, the group released its seventh full-length record, Faith In Our Time, a live album recorded in downtown Minneapolis. With a passion for writing songs that serve the local church and reach the global Church, the team continues to lead others in worship.
